# AYUSH support

HELIOS implements neutral capture for Ayurveda, Yoga/Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homoeopathy, other traditional systems and unknown. Records preserve system, reported use state, original name/statement, optional practitioner/facility, dates, reported reason/effect, source, evidence and verification status. They integrate with interview, documents, timeline, comparison, brief and doctor verification.

HELIOS does not validate practitioners, assert biomedical equivalence, infer interactions/causality/efficacy, or recommend AYUSH treatment. See [architecture](ayush/architecture.md), [data model](ayush/data-model.md), [provenance](ayush/provenance.md), [safety boundary](ayush/safety-boundary.md), and [limitations](ayush/limitations.md).
