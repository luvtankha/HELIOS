# Clinical glossary

The glossary is a versioned list of concept IDs, English terms, Hindi terms, synonyms, and transliterations. Concept identity is stable across languages. Entries are intentionally small and reviewable; adding a phrase requires a test for positive, negative, or ambiguous behavior.

The glossary is not a general dictionary and does not establish medical equivalence beyond the tested intake phrases. Medication names and dosages are never loosely translated.
