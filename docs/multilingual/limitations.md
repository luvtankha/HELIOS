# Limitations

English and Hindi support is limited to the implemented HELIOS workflow and approved deterministic resources; it is not universal or clinically validated translation. Code-switching coverage is phrase-based. Speech accuracy depends on the configured provider. Arbitrary clinical text falls back to its original form.

The future-language registry is informational only. TTS, RTL workflow QA, broad transliteration, automated medical translation, clinical safety decisions, and regulatory validation are not implemented. Five database integration suites remain environment-gated when PostgreSQL is unavailable.
