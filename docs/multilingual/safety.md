# Multilingual safety

Safety-critical meaning is carried by structured facts and explicit knowledge states, not translated prose. Negation, uncertainty, temporality, and provenance must survive every language boundary. Unsupported or unclear input triggers fallback or clarification.

Phase 12 does not implement the pending Phase 5 safety engine, triage, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Localized attention text is presentation only and must not be interpreted as a clinical risk score.
