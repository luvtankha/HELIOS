# HELIOS — 60-second pitch

“A consultation can begin with the patient reconstructing a story from memory: symptoms, old prescriptions, test reports, and what changed since the last visit. Language barriers and scattered documents make that harder. Yet the patient may already be waiting for an appointment.

HELIOS uses that waiting period for consented pre-consultation intake. The patient can speak or type in English or Hindi, answer questions selected by a deterministic interview engine, review extracted document facts, and check in for a real token. The same backend then prepares a source-labelled timeline, visit comparison, and short Clinical Brief.

The doctor opens a separate authorized workspace, reviews the evidence and uncertainty, and explicitly verifies or corrects facts. Optional AI adapters help transcribe or structure information; they do not make clinical or access-control decisions. HELIOS is a doctor-support workflow, not a diagnosis or prescription system. Its proposed clinical SafetyEngine remains future work.

Turn patient waiting time into clinical intelligence.”
