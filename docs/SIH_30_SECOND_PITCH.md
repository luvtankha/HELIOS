# HELIOS — 30-second pitch

“Patients often spend their waiting time preparing to repeat a fragmented story. HELIOS turns that time into structured clinical intelligence. With consent, they describe their concern in English or Hindi, answer adaptive questions, review document-derived facts, and receive a queue token. In a separate authorized workspace, the doctor sees source-linked history, what changed, and a concise brief before verifying the evidence. HELIOS organizes the story; it does not diagnose or replace the doctor.”

**Delivery note:** About 76 words; speak naturally. The default demo uses typed input, mock speech/OCR and rules-based interpretation. Do not describe the unimplemented SafetyEngine as active.
