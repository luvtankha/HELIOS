# AYUSH provenance

Origin and verification are independent. A patient-reported record remains `PATIENT_REPORTED` after doctor verification; a document-derived record remains `DOCUMENT_EXTRACTED`. `AYUSH_PRACTITIONER_DOCUMENTED` means only that the source document attributes the information to such a practitioner—it does not validate identity or registration.

Original patient/document wording, normalized display value, original language, translated/structured response, timestamps, document/page/region evidence, and the immutable DoctorVerification history remain separately traceable. Normalization never overwrites the original name or invents ingredients.
