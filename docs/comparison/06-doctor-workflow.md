# Doctor workflow

Open `/doctor/what-changed`, sign into the demo clinician account, select a patient and two chronologically ordered visits, then choose **Compare visits**. High-level counts precede change cards. Unchanged records are hidden from the default view and available via their own filter.

Cards show descriptive classification, explanation, before/after fields, and numeric delta where applicable. **Details & evidence** opens provenance, verification state, event date, document page, and source excerpt. A stale banner appears when a source timeline changed after generation.

Seeded Aarav Sharma compares 10 May with 1 September 2026: hemoglobin 10.4 → 8.9 g/dL, metformin 500 → 1000 mg, and breathing difficulty changing from `NOT_ASKED` to newly captured. All records are synthetic.
