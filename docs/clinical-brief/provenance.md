# Claim provenance

Every persisted claim requires at least one evidence reference and a source ID. References may identify patient/visit, interview-derived clinical history, symptom, medication, allergy, observation, timeline event, document/fact/page, comparison/change record, risk signal, doctor verification, or subsystem availability.

Doctor verified, patient confirmed, document extracted, patient reported, AI structured, and system generated are displayed as provenance labels. Their order is a display preference, not a universal medical reliability ranking. Confidence describes extraction quality, not medical certainty.
